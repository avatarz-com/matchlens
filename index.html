BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Real calculations. Honest limits.

These xG and xPass values are demonstration estimates.

Training, temperature calibration and evaluation use a fictional generator only. Neither model is validated or calibrated on real matches. No professional or proprietary model is used.

xPass

Pass completion estimate based on distance, forward progress, static lane blockers, receiver clearance and opponents near the passer.

xG if shot

A hypothetical immediate foot shot from the receiver’s decision-time position: goal distance, opening angle, shot-lane blockers and clearance. No actual shot is asserted.

Pass → shot

xPass × xG, assuming an immediate shot after a completed pass. Not a causal value or a complete possession model.

“xS” is ambiguous. MatchLens uses the explicit term xPass for pass completion.

For each model: 6,000 training examples, 1,500 calibration examples and 2,500 held-out test examples. Labels come from fictional probabilities defined in code. Replay outcomes never train these models.

Inputs, models, formulas, cutoff and missing data
The recorded result is reviewed after the action. Estimates always use the 6,000 ms snapshot, without the later pass resolution. Alternative replays are authored branches; they do not validate probabilities.
